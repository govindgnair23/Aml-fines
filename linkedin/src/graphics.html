A better design

What a deterrence system for AML could look like

Install the "speed camera" first: make failures visible, then respond quickly and in proportion.

1
Measure
Frequent, unannounced tests with synthetic transactions, run end to end through each bank.
Score detection and false positives, by typology
2
Respond fast
Modest, automatic consequences for poor scores, like a traffic ticket, not a fine a decade later.
Certainty over severity
3
Publish
Release scores or rating bands so peers, investors and correspondents can see them.
Publicity drives peer deterrence
4
Align pay
Defer executive pay beyond the detection lag; claw back on supervisory findings and poor test scores.
Reach decision-makers, not just shareholders
5
Escalate
Asset caps, business limits and individual bans follow automatically from repeated failure.
A credible "big gun", rarely used
Framework drawn from Ayres & Braithwaite's enforcement pyramid, Arlen & Kraakman on corporate liability, and Nagin on certainty vs severity.
